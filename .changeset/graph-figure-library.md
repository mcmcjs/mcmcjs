Draw a graph document as a black-and-white figure for papers with `figureTikz` and `figureSvg` from `@mcmcjs/doodleppl/figure`: stochastic nodes as circles, observed nodes shaded, deterministic nodes as double circles, constants as squares, plates as labelled boxes, and Greek names and indices typeset as maths. The figure keeps the positions saved in the graph, moving nodes and plates only as far as needed so nothing overlaps, and curving edges around nodes and labels in their way.
